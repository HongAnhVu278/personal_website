Thank you for applying (game)
-------------------
A little game I made about the job hunt. You cycle through applying, waiting, and getting rejected. There are two main meters, hope and savings, and the goal is to find a job before either of them runs out.
-------------------
Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, CSS, Phaser 3, and Tiled Map Editor
Demo: https://honganhvu278.github.io/thank-you-for-applying-game/
GitHub: https://github.com/HongAnhVu278/thank-you-for-applying-game
