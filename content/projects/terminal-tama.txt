Bitly: Terminal Tamagotchi
-------------------
A terminal pet that starves within three days unless fed by git commits, wired through a git-hook wrapper with JSON persistence.
-------------------
Tech: Go, Bubble Tea, Git hooks
Demo/Manual: https://honganhvu278.github.io/bitly-manual/
GitHub: https://github.com/HongAnhVu278/tamagotchi-tui
